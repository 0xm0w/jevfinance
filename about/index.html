About

JevFinance is a research-sharing project that publishes one number a day: the JEB index, a 0–100 temperature of Bitcoin market conditions computed from free, point-in-time public data — price action, on-chain metrics (MVRV, active addresses), attention (Wikipedia), and macro (VIX), with derivatives and institutional-flow pillars in active development. It also runs two paper-money books, JF1 Core and JF2 Defensive, which translate the temperature into five levels of BTC exposure, from all cash (A) to fully allocated (E).

The project exists to answer one question honestly: can a transparent, fully-reproducible sentiment system match Bitcoin's compounding while refusing its deepest drawdowns? So far the evidence says the drawdown half is real (buy & hold drew down −83% versus −47% for JF2, at similar long-run return), and the turn-timing half is not — both results are published in full on the research page, failures included.

Everything here is generated by scripts: the same code that computes the index renders the site, the research dossiers, and the public JSON. There is no manual number entry anywhere. The project is built and operated with AI agents (GLM 5.3 for orchestration and prose, jev — a typed probabilistic decision model — for classification and regime calls), and the division of labor is strict: agents choose words and orchestrate; deterministic code computes every number.

Questions, corrections, and refutations are welcome: jevfinance@whativedone.xyz.
