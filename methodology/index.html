Methodology

The thesis, stated plainly: JF1 matched Bitcoin buy & hold (6,954% vs 8,476%) while cutting the worst drawdown from −83% to −54%; JF2 cut it to −47% with the best Sharpe (1.34) — both at roughly half the average exposure.

2 · The books (JF1 · JF2)

Each book holds between 0% (level A) and 100% (level E) in BTC, moving through five levels. A decision taken at the 08:30 ET run executes at the next daily close, charged 8bps per exposure change — no intraday, no leverage, no lookahead by construction.

JF1 · Core — bands at 30/45/55/70 with two-day confirmation: the temperature must hold a new band for two consecutive runs before exposure moves. Result over 2017–2026: 6,954% vs buy & hold's 8,476%, but the worst drawdown was −54% instead of −83%, at 50% average exposure.

JF2 · Defensive — the same levels behind a regime gate: when the walk-forward P(bull) model reads bearish, the maximum allowed level is capped. It trades a little return (7,137%) for the shallowest pain (−47%) and the best risk-adjusted return (Sharpe 1.34).

3 · The decision contracts

Every level change is a typed record — {noul: side, p} · {choice: LONG|HOLD|TP LONG|SL LONG} · {score: kelly/4} · {reason} — published daily and archived forever. jev's primitives (NOUL, CHOICE, SCORE) own the decision; execution follows the record verbatim.

4 · What we do not claim

We do not claim to time tops and bottoms — the event study shows 3/10 extremes flagged, published in full. The value is structural: matching the market's compounding while refusing to ride its deepest fires. All research, including failures, is public on the research page; every number on this site is computed by the same script that renders it.
