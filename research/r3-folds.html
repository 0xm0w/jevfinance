Where jev is — and where jev shines

Verifications used in this dossier (jev typed contracts, run today as deterministic stand-ins — at M2 the identical contracts execute via live jev calls with probability gates): (1) schema gate — every snapshot row passes a typed schema before scoring; malformed source payloads are rejected to neutral, never averaged in; (2) regime task — the P(bull) line is exactly jev's {regime: bull|bear|chop, probabilities} contract; this backtest is the accuracy bound the live model must beat; (3) calibration check — probability outputs are stretched/calibrated on train windows and never consumed raw; (4) anomaly triage — 3σ jumps in the series are flagged with a typed {likely_cause, action} rather than silently smoothed.

Where jev shines here: the historical layers (price, MVRV, attention counts) are deterministic and jev adds nothing there — that is deliberate. jev is load-bearing exactly where data is unstructured text: classifying thousands of headlines and Reddit posts per run into typed sentiment with probabilities (pillar D's live version will be far richer than the attention proxy testable retroactively), tagging divergence causes (news-driven vs leverage flush vs data issue), triaging anomalous readings before they touch the index, and producing the fact-bound regime line. The research shows why the typed-probability shape matters: uncalibrated, free-text sentiment would have been swept into the index with the wrong orientation (attention inverts sign by regime). jev's contract — typed output, probabilities attached, gated, capped at 12 index points — is what made every failure above detectable and every discovery reproducible.

Limitations

Overlapping forward returns inflate apparent significance (no transaction-cost or strategy claims are made); pillar D is an attention proxy — retro-classified news/Reddit sentiment does not exist, so live D may test differently; funding history (167d) and ETF flows (2024→) are too short to backtest; three folds + feature drop-outs reduce but do not eliminate multiple-testing bias; ground-truth "bear" is defined by moving-average rules, not exogenous truth.
