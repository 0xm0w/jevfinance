The honest reading: carry is positive and extremely stable — the strict 15%/7.5% band ran at Sharpe ~3 with 0.8% max drawdown — but the absolute return is small (~0.5–2%/yr on Binance alone at daily capture). The 2021 manias did pay big; the 2023–26 regime pays savings-account rates. This is a cash-plus instrument, not an alpha engine.

Where this leaves the search: three edges tested, three answers. Predictive daily sentiment — no edge. Drawdown structuring (A–E levels) — real edge: +809% vs +657% for buy-&-hold with 45% vs 77% drawdown. Funding carry — positive, stable, tiny on one exchange at daily capture. The synthesis is the actual product: the A–E system holds cash roughly half the time, and SCORE can park that cash in the strict-band carry sleeve — turning the level system's biggest idle asset into a yield stream with ~1% drawdown. NOUL/CHOICE/SCORE run both books with the same typed contracts.
